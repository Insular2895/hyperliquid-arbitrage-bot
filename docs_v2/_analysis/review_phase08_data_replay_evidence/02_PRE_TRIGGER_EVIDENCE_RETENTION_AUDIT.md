# PRE-Trigger Evidence Retention Audit

Before T0, enabled recent evidence remains provisionally available for a calibrated horizon. At T0 the execution/incident identifies scope, pins the relevant immutable PRE range, promotes retention metadata to P1, captures active/POST, and closes the bundle. Pressure may degrade optional evidence visibly. If part of PRE is gone, exact gaps/reasons/scope are recorded and use becomes `LOW_FIDELITY` or `INVALID_FOR_REPLAY`. No exact seconds or buffer technology is fixed.
