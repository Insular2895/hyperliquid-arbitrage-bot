# QF-027 versus QF-076 Audit

| Formula | Question answered | Inputs | Authority |
|---|---|---|---|
| QF-027 Max Profitable Size | largest q satisfying its profitability condition | canonical execution-aware economics in formula scope | economic candidate bound |
| QF-076 Validated Capacity | largest q for which every required gate is true | economics, Risk, capital, evidence support, tails, model/fidelity/operations | maximum admissible evidence-backed q |

QF-027 can exceed QF-076 when profitability is modeled beyond validated support. QF-076 can be constrained by Risk, capital, OOD, fidelity or operational limits even when QF-027 is high. Neither is multiplied by completion probability. A selected q must satisfy both relevant economic and validated-capacity constraints.
