# Slippage, Impact and Adverse-Selection Audit

QF-016 full-depth NetConvert is the executable cashflow owner for fees and immediate book walk/slippage. QF-042 mechanical impact and L2 slippage may diagnose the same immediate liquidity consumption; they are not two additional independent cash deductions when NetConvert already embodies that walk.

Shadow Book mutation represents mechanical consumption in simulated paths. A participant-response model may separately represent exogenous liquidity movement caused after the action, and maker adverse selection may price conditional post-fill movement. Those distinct effects require explicit horizon, conditioning and evidence.

Each scenario declares whether a term is a cashflow component, state transition, diagnostic feature or external penalty. One observed price movement cannot be fully charged to several causes.
