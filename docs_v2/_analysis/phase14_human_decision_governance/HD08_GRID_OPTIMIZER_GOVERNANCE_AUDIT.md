# HD-08 Grid and Optimizer Governance Audit

Grid/refinement resolution and solver/tie/failure representation are calibration or implementation choices under deterministic parity. Bounded enumeration is the safe baseline. Evidence may make a more complex optimizer eligible; a scoped human approval may promote it, but cannot rescue failed parity, unsupported q or absent EconomicLift. Historical HD-08 is not a manual grid-value decision.
