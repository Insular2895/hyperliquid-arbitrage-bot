# HD-03 Model Governance Audit

Data and the declared temporal OOS/censor-aware process select eligible artifacts, coefficients, horizons, cohorts, clipping and hyperparameters. Humans may approve, narrow, defer or reject promotion of an eligible candidate; they cannot promote an ineligible candidate or call inferior evidence superior. CapabilityManifest, readiness, OOD and Risk remain independent gates. Historical HD-03 is therefore reclassified into `LEARNED` internals plus a scoped promotion record, not a parameter-choice family.
