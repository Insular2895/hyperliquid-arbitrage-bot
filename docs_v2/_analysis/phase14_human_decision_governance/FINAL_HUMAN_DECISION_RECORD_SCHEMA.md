# Final Human Decision Record Schema

Required fields: decision ID/version; question and classification; primary owner; consulted domains; exact capability/market/mode/q/model/infra/build scope; candidate choices; evidence IDs and eligibility result; policy rationale; `APPROVE|NARROW|DEFER|REJECT|REQUEST_MORE_EVIDENCE`; restrictions; non-overridable gates; validity/review triggers; fallback; consumers/blocker scope; approver/time; supersession link. It grants no authority outside the effective permission intersection.
