# Human Approval Ceiling Contract

```text
Effective promotion = EvidenceEligibility
                    ∩ HumanPolicyApproval
                    ∩ CapabilityManifest
                    ∩ CurrentReadiness
                    ∩ RiskPermission
```

Reviewers may approve, narrow, defer, reject or request evidence. They may not override failed/stale/invalid evidence, Risk, OOD, unsupported q, missing calibration, invalid mathematics or external reality. An approval is scoped, versioned, evidence-linked, expiring/reviewable and independently reversible by safety demotion.
