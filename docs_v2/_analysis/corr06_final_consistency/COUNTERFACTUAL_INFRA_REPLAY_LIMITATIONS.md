# Counterfactual Infrastructure Replay Limitations

## Strong uses

Recorded data can support opportunity/BBO/L2 economics, edge survival, actual paired arrival/stability, local compute timing, counterfactual latency and semantic-preserving implementation A/B tests. FullL2/FastL1 or representation/compiler/container variants require identical economic results unless timing-dependent behavior is intentionally versioned.

## Claims it cannot establish alone

Passive Replay cannot prove actual IOC fill or partial probability, route completion, Recovery result, write-priority capture benefit or Live account/exchange behavior. Our order participates in sequencing and matching. Shadow remains non-actual. Micro-live/Live actual evidence overrides a contradictory simulated claim and triggers recalibration/demotion.

An old profile applied to a new Dataset predicts under its stated model/scope; it does not mean the candidate truly received each event at the reconstructed time. RAW is unchanged. Profile staleness, support gaps, p2p topology and correlated latency are disclosed. Counterfactual outcomes never enter actual PnL or fill ledgers.
