# Micro-live Validation Contract

DOCUMENTATION STATUS:
REBUILD IN PROGRESS

Micro-live is the Live engine with real market, real transport, real fills and real account, constrained by small calibrated capital/size/frequency and narrow market/mode scope. The historical EUR 40–50 example is a calibration probe, not a universal threshold or business phase.

Before start: M3 evidence is current; exact manifest scope is declared; limits/stop conditions and predicted metrics are frozen; account/book/clock/infra are reconciled; safe stop, UNKNOWN, Recovery and incident procedures are ready; operator approval is recorded.

For every attempt retain prediction and actuality for send/ACK/first/last fill latency, fill/partial/zero/cancel status, time/quantity, execution price/slippage/fees, post-trade response, Recovery path/loss, PnL and final reconciliation. Retain rejects and failed attempts.

Micro-live is not optimized to earn PnL. It intervenes to test assumptions that Replay/Shadow cannot. Evidence at one size, market, regime or mode does not authorize a larger band, another market or maker mode. Stop immediately on a locked safety condition; statistical underperformance follows the declared drift/demotion policy rather than arbitrary reaction to a few trades.
