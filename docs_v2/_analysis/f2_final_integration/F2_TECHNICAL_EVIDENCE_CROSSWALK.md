Scientific evidence can reject, narrow or make a candidate eligible for the next review. It cannot
create M-level promotion, Strategy activation, `ValidatedQSet` membership, `Q_validated`, Risk
permission or capital authorization. A stage exit, technical phase exit and implementation
authorization remain three separate records.
