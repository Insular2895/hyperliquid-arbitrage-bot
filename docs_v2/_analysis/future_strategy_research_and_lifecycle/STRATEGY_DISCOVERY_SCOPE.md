# Strategy Discovery Scope

Strategy Discovery is `FUTURE / DEFERRED_BLOCKED`. It may begin only after the complete baseline gate is evidenced, including deterministic Replay, canonical Simulator, Shadow, reconciled Micro-live outcome evidence and deterministic reporting.

It explores hypotheses and candidates within the same Core. It owns no market/account truth, Risk gate, capacity authorization, ESM transition, Recovery action, accounting entry or Live promotion. Its output is a versioned candidate and evidence package.

Initial research should favor interpretable baseline comparisons. Regime portfolios, learned candidate generation and autonomous research orchestration are later research topics, not initial requirements or promised production capabilities.
