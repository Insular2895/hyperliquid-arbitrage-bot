# StrategySpec Contract

A `StrategySpec` freezes family/version/lineage, scope, observations/features, entry/abstention/sizing/exit policies, parameter domain, dependencies, evidence, limitations and fallback. It can propose q and behavior but cannot authorize either; Risk and Execution remain authoritative.

Material changes create new versions. Status never substitutes for phase, human, Risk or capital authorization. Full details: [StrategySpec and Lineage](../../deep-specs/strategy-research/03_STRATEGY_SPEC_AND_LINEAGE.md).
