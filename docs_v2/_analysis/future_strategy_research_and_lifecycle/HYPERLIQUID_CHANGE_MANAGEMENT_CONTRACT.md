# Hyperliquid Change-Management Contract

A detected exchange/API/WS/order/fee/precision/metadata/node/priority or SDK change creates dated `EXT-*` evidence, affected-artifact analysis, fixtures and a revalidation issue. It never silently rewrites Formula, Risk, StrategySpec, Simulator calibration or production configuration.

If compatibility or economic truth is uncertain, the affected capability becomes NON-READY or scope/research-only, preserves evidence, and requires schema/economic/replay/shadow/micro-live validation appropriate to impact. Rollback uses a known compatible signed artifact and does not erase actual history.
