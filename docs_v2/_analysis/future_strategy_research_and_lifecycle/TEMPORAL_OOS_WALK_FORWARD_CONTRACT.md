# Temporal OOS and Walk-Forward Contract

Discovery/training, validation and final temporal holdout are separate. Walk-forward windows and refit rules are frozen before evaluation. Overlapping labels/features require purging and a justified embargo. Random row splits cannot support time-dependent promotion claims.

The final holdout is not a tuning surface. Reuse requires a new decision family and later untouched period. Full details: [Temporal OOS and Walk-Forward](../../deep-specs/strategy-research/06_TEMPORAL_OOS_AND_WALK_FORWARD.md).
