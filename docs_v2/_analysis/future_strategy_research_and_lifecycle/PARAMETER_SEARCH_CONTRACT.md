# Parameter Search Contract

Search starts from a falsifiable hypothesis and a bounded `StrategySpec`. Manual/grid/random/Latin-hypercube/Bayesian/evolutionary methods form an evidence-justified progression, not a maturity claim. All attempts, adaptive choices, failures and costs are retained.

Risk hard gates, kill switches, freshness, Recovery/Reconciliation, capital ceilings and execution safety are locked—not search dimensions. Selection favors robust stable regions and then proceeds to untouched temporal OOS, not directly to promotion.
