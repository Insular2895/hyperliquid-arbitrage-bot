# Experiment Reporting Contract

Each report binds identity/version/status, hypothesis and falsifier, owner/reviewer, dates/scope, Dataset/Run manifests, code/config/formula/model/strategy versions, temporal split, leakage/multiplicity policy, method/search/seed/budget/stopping rule, metrics/guardrails, results/uncertainty, failures/censoring/missingness, sensitivity/OOS/stress, baseline comparison, claim classes, limits, disposition and evidence links.

A future deterministic generator owns manifest values, hashes, counts and measured outputs. Human and AI sections are additive and cannot overwrite machine facts. Corrections supersede rather than edit history. Templates live in [`docs_v2/research/templates`](../../research/templates/EXPERIMENT_REPORT_TEMPLATE.md).
