# AI Research Authority Boundary

AI may search approved evidence, classify claims, propose hypotheses, draft experiment plans, detect possible anomalies, summarize results and recommend research or demotion for review.

AI may not:

- promote/demote a strategy or model;
- change Risk rules, capital limits, q, kill switches or Live thresholds;
- issue capital-bearing execution commands;
- relabel simulated outcomes as actual;
- overwrite machine-generated report facts;
- self-train or self-modify a Live artifact;
- suppress failed/negative evidence.

AI output defaults to `HYPOTHESIS` or `INTERPRETATION` unless a deterministic tool produces a separately identified machine fact. A human-controlled workflow owns material decisions.
