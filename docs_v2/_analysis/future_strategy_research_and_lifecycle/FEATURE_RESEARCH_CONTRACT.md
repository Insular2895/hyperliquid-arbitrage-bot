# Feature Research Contract

Features require versioned definitions, sources, units, event/availability timing, lookback/warm-up, missingness, support, lineage, point-in-time proof and maturity. No reconciled/future label may leak into the decision-time feature set.

Feature status is separate from strategy status and Live permission. Source/schema changes trigger revalidation. Full details: [Feature Registry](../../deep-specs/strategy-research/02_FEATURE_REGISTRY.md).
