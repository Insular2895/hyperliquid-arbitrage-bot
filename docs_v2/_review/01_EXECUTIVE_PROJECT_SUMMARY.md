# Executive Project Summary

`DOCUMENTATION STATUS: AWAITING HUMAN REVIEW`

## What the product is

This is a Hyperliquid spot arbitrage and routing control system. It observes exchange truth, reconstructs coherent state, evaluates size-dependent executable conversions, reserves shared resources, applies constitutional Risk, executes statefully, reconciles actual exchange outcomes and earns capability one evidence-bounded scope at a time.

The production Core is a Rust modular monolith with a single logical ordered commit boundary. Python is an offline research, calibration and reporting environment. C++ is not part of the baseline.

## Current V1

- Same-venue Hyperliquid spot only.
- `OWA`: A→X→B is compared with a fair direct A→B conversion using the same input, terminal asset and conventions.
- `Triangle`: A→X→B→A returns to its starting asset.
- `TT` is the first normal capital-bearing execution mode after Replay, Shadow and bounded Micro-live evidence.
- `TTT` follows TT and needs separate three-leg, intermediate-exposure and Recovery evidence.
- `NetConvert(q)` is the sole canonical executable conversion primitive.
- Risk, reservations, actual-fill Inventory, Recovery, Reconciliation, Recorder/Replay, Atlas and evidence governance are mandatory system components.

## Later V1 and beyond

`MT` and `MTT` are later-V1 maker-led modes and need maker fill/time/adverse-selection evidence. Participant models describe collective survival, liquidity response and competition; they do not invent individual identities. The Counterfactual Simulator progresses from F0 historical and F1 latency/mechanical distributions to evidence-backed F2 queue and F3 response behavior. F4 interactive agents remain Research.

The Market Atlas records structural and empirical support. Capital Reachability says where usable capital can reach; Terminal Viability tests post-action inventory and exits; Position Sizing selects total supported exposure; Slicing only decomposes it. Bridge compares relocation paths with `STAY` and is never an OWA substitute.

## Safety and evidence posture

Unknown or stale state cannot open new risk. Resources are reserved before order effects. `SENT` may have executed, `UNKNOWN` keeps reservations locked, and cancel requested is not canceled. Only unique actual fills change economic state. Partial fills immediately change Inventory and force re-evaluation. Recovery optimizes from current exposure without honoring sunk route costs; Reconciliation re-establishes orders, fills and balances truth.

Replay and Live share the same Core. A decision is reproducible from ordered events, resolved configuration, pinned model artifacts, formula version and explicit seed. Every capital permission is bounded by a current `CapabilityManifest`, maturity, `Q_validated`, operational readiness and Risk.
