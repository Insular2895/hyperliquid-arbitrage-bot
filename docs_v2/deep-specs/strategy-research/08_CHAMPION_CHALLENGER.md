# 08 — Champion/Challenger

STATUS: FUTURE SPECIFIED / LAST-LAYER ACTIVATION / NOT ACTIVE

Champion/Challenger is a controlled evidence comparison, not automatic strategy replacement. Exactly one economic execution owner remains active unless a separately isolated capital allocation is explicitly authorized.

Progression:

1. frozen baseline and challenger specs;
2. deterministic Replay parity and OOS evidence;
3. Monte Carlo/stress rejection gate;
4. Shadow comparison on the same eligible populations;
5. separately authorized bounded Micro-live evidence;
6. human promotion, scope or rejection decision;
7. monitored champion with a declared rollback/fallback.

The comparison contract freezes population, support, q, economics, cost ownership, guardrails, minimum evidence, uncertainty, stopping rules and tie policy. A challenger may win on a declared multi-objective basis only if no safety/reliability guardrail regresses materially. Peak PnL, latency or completion alone is insufficient.

Promotion evidence binds Replay determinism, Simulator/Monte Carlo robustness, temporal OOS/walk-forward, sensitivity, multiplicity disposition, support/OOD, Shadow comparison, actual Micro-live predicted-versus-realized fills/latency/fees/priority/Recovery/PnL/tails where required, validated q scope, rollback and reviewer. A challenger may remain rejected/held even when one metric is better; the incumbent remains champion unless the complete declared case is stronger.

Automatic promotion is forbidden. AI may summarize or propose the decision and must expose evidence/uncertainty, but cannot replace the reviewer, change Risk, change capital, alter q/Live thresholds or issue a promotion command.

Demotion is safe and monotone: stop new risk if required, preserve the ESM owner, let Recovery/Reconciliation resolve truth, return to a known supported artifact or NON-READY, and emit evidence. Rollback never rewrites prior actual outcomes.

Several validated strategies may coexist as dormant scoped artifacts. Regime-based portfolios remain still later: selection must be point-in-time, every member independently validated, capital/Risk shared constraints preserved and switching unable to bypass promotion.
