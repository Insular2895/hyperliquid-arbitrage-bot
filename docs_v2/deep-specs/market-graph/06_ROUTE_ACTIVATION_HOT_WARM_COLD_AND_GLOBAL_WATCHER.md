## Three independent questions

- Graph: does a structural conversion/route exist?
- HWC/Activation: how much current compute/evidence should it receive?
- Risk: may a proposed action proceed?

No answer substitutes for another.

## Global Watcher

Global Watcher retains cheap awareness over every eligible structural market: topology/status, BBO/freshness and bounded anomaly/promotion signals. It proposes tier/activation review; it does not trade, move capital or alter Formula definitions.

## Tiers

HOT receives full supported L2, route features and bounded forecasts for capital-reachable/productive routes. WARM increases L2/features/Bridge analysis to confirm emerging or adjacent utility. COLD retains minimal BBO/timestamp/state and Recorder evidence. COLD is not ignored; HOT is not safe-by-definition.

Promotion inputs may include capital reachability, adjacency, persistent Atlas changes, liquidity/opportunity anomaly, recent support and strategy/model capability. Demotion requires governed contrary evidence. Hysteresis uses different promotion/demotion evidence to prevent churn; numeric examples from source are illustrative and thresholds remain calibrated/learned.

## Activation

Route Activation selects a subset of structurally valid routes for expensive evaluation. It is reversible and versioned. Capital movement can change reachability/HWC but cannot change topology. Risk/capability can deactivate work but a high Atlas score cannot restore forbidden execution.

## Exploration control

Broad Recorder/Watcher coverage preserves discovery in WARM/COLD and prevents capital placement from training only on already-selected regions. Detected, rejected, accepted and captured opportunity episodes remain distinct. See [HWC matrix](../../_analysis/pass08_graph_routes_quant/HOT_WARM_COLD_MATRIX.md).
