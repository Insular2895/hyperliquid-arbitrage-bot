## TTT ordering resolution

Basic OWA/Triangle economics precede sophisticated Participant models so episodes and baselines exist. TTT detection, Replay and Shadow therefore do not wait for advanced models. Normal real TTT follows validated TT and its own three-leg/actual-output/intermediate/Recovery evidence. If a particular TTT configuration consumes a Participant forecast, that model becomes a critical dependency through its CapabilityManifest; otherwise it is not a universal gate.

## Maker sequencing

MT and MTT follow Maker Intelligence. The order type may exist long before the probability of fill, time-to-fill, adverse selection, cancellation/expiry and remaining-leg economics are calibrated. `TM` and `MM` remain type-supported/default-disabled and outside the initial activation sequence.

## Portfolio and Bridge

Portfolio allocation begins only after independent candidate validity. It cannot double-consume a shared balance or book. Bridge comes later because it requires a history of future opportunities, terminal viability, exits and capital utilization. It compares `EV_destination` and `EV_stay` minus Bridge/Exit/Relocation Risk costs; it is not arbitrage.

## Scaling modes

- **Horizontal:** add genuinely independent validated opportunities while respecting shared constraints.
- **Vertical:** expand q only into a larger supported `Q_validated` band.
- **Market/strategy/maker:** repeat evidence for the added scope.
- **Capital relocation:** separately validate Bridge and destination utility.
- **Infrastructure:** benchmark early, promote only from robust attributable economics.

Preferred early direction is horizontal before excessive vertical size because one book has finite depth/capacity/survival, but this remains an evidence-rebuttable heuristic. Scale-down, disable, revert and demote are normal outputs.
