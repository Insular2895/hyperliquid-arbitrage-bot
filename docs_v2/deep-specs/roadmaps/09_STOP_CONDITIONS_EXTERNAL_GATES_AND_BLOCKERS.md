## Three distinct controls

- A **stop condition** is observed invalidity or failure that prevents affected promotion/operation.
- An **external revalidation gate** is a current fact that must be verified before the affected contract is implemented or activated.
- An **implementation blocker** is an unresolved dependency that prevents a specific phase/capability, not necessarily the project.

## Scope rule

Failures are global, market-, strategy-, mode-, model-, size- or infrastructure-scoped. Use the smallest scope that is provably safe. If ownership/account truth or a shared dependency cannot be isolated, scope widens. Safe cancel, reconciliation, evidence preservation and bounded risk-reducing Recovery remain where possible.

## Capital-critical minimum

No Micro-live when any of these is unproven: reliable Book/Recorder, current fees/precision/order rules, hard Risk invariants, actual-fill inventory/reservations, ESM UNKNOWN/cancel handling, protected transport, Recovery, Reconciliation, safe deployment/single owner, monitoring/alerts, safe shutdown and rollback, M3 Shadow, predeclared predicted↔actual joins.

## Non-blocking advanced work

F4 agents, private node, hot standby, full cross-exchange, dense response models, complex portfolio optimizer and TM/MM do not block initial TT. They remain Research/Future or mode-scoped gates.
