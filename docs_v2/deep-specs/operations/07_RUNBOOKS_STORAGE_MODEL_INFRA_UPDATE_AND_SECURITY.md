## Storage/Recorder

Protect pinned/critical evidence, apply priority/backpressure policy, account for invalid/lost ranges, remediate capacity/write/fsync/backlog, escalate Risk when critical persistence is impossible, and verify stable drain/quality before resume.

## Model/Simulator drift

Scope dependent capabilities, preserve predictions/outcomes/support slices, fallback/reduce/disable, check data/features/artifact, reproduce point-in-time, recalibrate offline, rerun temporal OOS/Shadow/Micro-live and explicitly re-promote.

## Infrastructure/clock/network

Degrade through Risk; preserve latency/route/resource/clock evidence; repair/resync/reconnect; rebuild feed/book and reconcile if state could be missed; verify sustained distributions rather than one good sample.

## Update/license

Keep current verified owner if failure precedes stop; otherwise restore the known compatible digest/migration path. Never rewind exchange truth. License failure blocks new risk without blocking cancel/reconcile/Recovery/read. Restart non-ready.

## Secret/owner compromise

Risk-off, fence/revoke/rotate, preserve sanitized evidence, audit unauthorized effects, rebuild from trusted artifacts, prove exactly one owner and reconcile the account. Security approval and capability revalidation precede resume.

## CORR-04 feed/node profile response

On node/challenger lag, gap, mismatch, disconnect or shared-resource pressure, isolate the challenger first; if canonical state is affected, block affected new risk, preserve evidence, rebuild/resync and reconcile before readiness. A feed-profile rollback or container/network/CPU/kernel change is versioned and never hot-fused. Speculative-lane failure only discards research work unless it harms a shared safety dependency.
